Support

Partner portal · Farmer mobile app

Need help signing in, managing loans or insurance, or using the farmer app? Reach our team and we will point you to the right next step.

Email

support@agritrust.rw

Include your organisation name, role (bank, insurer, admin, farmer), and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or MoMo PINs.

Farmers

Download the Agri Trust mobile app from Google Play. Loan repayments and farm records are handled in the app, not on this partner portal.

Partners

Banks, insurers, and officers use this web portal. If you need an account, ask your Agri Trust administrator to invite you — public self-registration is for farmers only.
