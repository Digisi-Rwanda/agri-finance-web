Request data deletion

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to the Hinga mobile app

This page explains how to delete some or all of your personal data in Hinga without deleting your account, and how to permanently remove your whole account. The Agri Trust Partner Portal (this website) is for bank and insurer staff—partner account deletion is handled by your organisation administrator.

Delete specific data (keep your Hinga account)

  1. Delete a farm — open Farms, select the farm, choose Delete farm.
  2. Remove your profile photo — open Profile, tap your photo, remove it.
  3. Delete notifications — on Home, delete individual alerts.
  4. Stop sharing data — open Profile → Sharing & privacy and turn off transaction, satellite/location, or bank/lender sharing.

Request deletion of other data by email

For data you cannot remove in the app (transactions, yield history, national ID images):

  1. Email from the address linked to your Hinga account: support@agritrust.rw
  2. Subject: Hinga — data deletion request
  3. Include your full name, phone number, and what you want deleted.
  4. We respond within 30 days.

Delete your entire Hinga account

  1. Open Profile in the Hinga app.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

If in-app deletion fails, email support@agritrust.rw with subject Hinga — account deletion.

What is deleted vs kept

Partial deletion in the app

  • Deleted: the farm, photo, or notification you remove.
  • Kept: your account, other farms, transactions, credit history, and loan or insurance records not tied to that item.

Full account deletion

  • Deleted: profile, farms, app-stored photos, and most server data.
  • May be kept: records required by law, fraud prevention, or an active loan or insurance policy until it ends.

Retention: backup copies may remain up to 90 days, then are removed unless law or an active financial product requires longer retention.
