Request data deletion

Hinga by Agri Trust · Last updated: 2 September 2026

This page explains how to delete some or all of your personal data in the Hinga mobile app without deleting your account, and how to permanently delete your whole account if you choose.

Delete specific data (keep your account)

You can remove the following data yourself inside the Hinga app:

  1. Delete a farm — open the Farms tab, select the farm, and choose Delete farm. This removes that farm’s name, crops, location, and related records from your account.
  2. Remove your profile photo — open Profile (Settings), tap your photo, and choose to remove it.
  3. Delete notifications — on the Home screen, open your notifications and delete individual alerts.
  4. Stop sharing data — open Profile → Sharing & privacy and turn off transaction, satellite/location, or bank/lender sharing. This stops new sharing with partners; it does not erase all historical records already used for credit or products.

Request deletion of other data by email

If you want specific data removed that you cannot delete in the app (for example certain transaction records, yield history, or national ID images), email us:

  1. Send a message from the email address linked to your Hinga account to support@agritrust.rw.
  2. Use the subject line: Hinga — data deletion request.
  3. Include your full name, phone number, and the types of data you want deleted.
  4. We will confirm your identity and respond within 30 days.

Delete your entire account

To permanently delete your Hinga account and most associated data:

  1. Open Profile (Settings) in the app.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

If in-app deletion fails, email support@agritrust.rw with the subject Hinga — account deletion and we will process your request.

What is deleted vs kept

When you delete specific items in the app

  • Deleted: the farm, profile photo, or notification you remove.
  • Kept: your account, other farms, transactions, credit history, and loan or insurance records not tied to the item you deleted.

When you delete your whole account

  • Deleted: your profile, farms, app-stored photos, and most app data on our servers.
  • May be kept: records required by law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or an active loan or insurance policy until the obligation ends. Payment references from mobile-money partners may also be retained as required by financial regulations.

Retention period: after deletion, backup copies may remain for up to 90 days for security and recovery, then are removed unless law or an active financial product requires longer retention.
