Last updated: September 2026 · Operated by Agri Trust in Rwanda

Hinga — farmer mobile app

Android app for farmers: farms, yields, credit profile, loans, insurance, MoMo repayments, and optional weather or AI tools.

Agri Trust Partner Portal

This website for invited banks, insurers, cooperatives, and officers — review applications, manage products, and operate workflows farmers start in Hinga.

Hinga is your digital farm and finance companion. Agri Trust operates the platform behind Hinga and the partner portal. This policy explains what we collect, why, who may see it, and the choices you have.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information you give us

  • Account: name, email, phone, password (stored hashed), username, and—for farmers—national ID details and photos you upload.
  • Farms: farm name, crops, size, photos, administrative location, and optional GPS coordinates.
  • Money records: sales, expenses, and mobile-money references you log or confirm.
  • Credit & products: credit score factors, loan and insurance applications, repayments, and consent records.
  • Partner staff: organisation name, role (bank, insurer, admin, etc.), and work activity in the portal.

2. Credit score & financial products

We combine farm data, yields, and transactions to build a credit profile shown in Hinga. Banks and insurers receive only what they need when a farmer applies for a product and agrees to share—or when partner staff access records within their assigned role on the portal.

3. Loan repayments (MTN / Airtel MoMo)

When a farmer repays through Hinga, payment is processed by our licensed partner Paypack. We receive payment status and references—we never ask for or store a MoMo PIN.

4. Optional smart tools (Hinga)

Rain and crop-stress estimates use public weather data and farm location only if satellite/location sharing is on. The farming assistant may send your question to our server; if AI is enabled, a language service helps answer in your language. You can use Hinga without these tools.

5. You control sharing (Hinga farmers)

In the Hinga app, open Profile → Sharing & privacy to allow or stop:

  • using transactions for credit;
  • location-based rain and crop estimates;
  • sharing with banks/lenders when you apply for finance.

6. Security & service partners

Passwords are hashed; sign-in uses secure tokens over HTTPS. Trusted partners host the platform, send email/SMS, process payments, and supply weather or AI services only to run Hinga and Agri Trust—not for their own marketing.

7. Your rights & deletion

  • Update profile and farm data in Hinga anytime.
  • Delete farms, profile photos, or notifications without closing your account.
  • Delete your whole Hinga account under Profile → Delete account.
  • See our dedicated data deletion guide for step-by-step instructions.
  • Email us for access, correction, or deletion requests—we may retain records while a loan is active or the law requires.

8. Children

Hinga and the partner portal are for adults who farm or work with farmers. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product or the law changes. The “Last updated” date above will change; material changes will be highlighted in-product or by email when practical.
