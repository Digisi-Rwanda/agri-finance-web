Agri Trust is your digital farm and finance companion: record fields and sales, see your credit profile, apply for loans and crop insurance, and repay by mobile money. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information you give us

Name, phone, and email for your account; farm details (crops, size, photos, district or GPS if you choose); money records you log; loan and insurance applications; and your yes/no choices for sharing data.

2. Credit score & financial products

We combine your farm data, yields, and transactions to build a credit profile and risk band shown in the app. Banks and insurers only receive what they need when you apply for a product and agree to share.

3. Loan repayments (MTN / Airtel MoMo)

When you pay through the app, repayment is processed by our licensed partner Paypack. We receive payment status and references—we never ask for or store your MoMo PIN.

4. Optional smart tools

Farm rain & crop stress uses public weather data and your farm location (only if you turn on satellite/location sharing). The farming assistant may send your question to our secure server; if AI is enabled, a language service helps answer in your language. You can use the app without these tools.

5. You control sharing

In the mobile app, open Profile → Sharing & privacy to allow or stop: (a) using transactions for credit, (b) location-based rain and crop estimates, (c) sharing with banks/lenders. Some steps need consent to continue.

6. Security & service partners

Passwords are hashed; sign-in uses secure tokens. Trusted partners host the app, send email/SMS, process payments, and supply weather or AI services only to run Agri Trust—not for their own marketing.

7. Your rights

Update your profile anytime, revoke consent in Settings, and contact us about access or deletion. We may keep certain records while a loan is active or the law requires.

8. Children

Agri Trust is intended for adults who farm or work with farmers. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product or the law changes. The “Last updated” date above will change; material changes will be highlighted in-product or by email when practical.
